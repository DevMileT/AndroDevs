Help

How to play

Choose your bet, from 10 to 100 coins, with the bet buttons and press Spin. The three reels stop and the prize depends on the combination.

  • Win: the prize is greater than your bet.
  • Draw: you get exactly your bet back.
  • Loss: there is no prize and you lose your bet.

Prizes

Three sevensx10
Three bellsx5
Three of any other symbol (cherry, lemon, grape or orange)x3
Exactly two sevens, two bells or two cherriesx2
Exactly one cherry (your bet is returned)x1
Any other combinationx0

Daily bonus

Once a day you can claim 50 coins from the Menu. If your balance is below 10 you cannot bet: claim the bonus or wait until the next day. The bonus depends on the device's date.

When you win

When you win you can save a screenshot to the gallery and add the win to your calendar. The first time, the app will explain why it asks for permissions.

Privacy

  • Lucky Sevens uses virtual coins only: there is no real money and there are no purchases.
  • If you allow it, your approximate location is saved when you win. It is stored only on your device, is not sent anywhere and is excluded from backups.
  • This help is inside the app and does not need a connection.
